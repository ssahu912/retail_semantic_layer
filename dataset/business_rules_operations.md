# Burger Haven - Business Rules & Operational Constraints
## Quick Service Restaurant Chain - Riyadh Operations

---

## 1. STORE OPERATIONS

### 1.1 Operating Hours
- **Standard Stores**: 08:00 - 23:30 (Sunday - Thursday)
- **Weekend Hours**: 08:00 - 00:30 (Friday - Saturday)
- **Ramadan Hours**: 20:00 - 03:00 (adjusted for Iftar/Suhoor)
- **Prayer Times**: Operations continue but may experience 10-15 min slowdowns during prayer times

### 1.2 Staffing Requirements
- **Minimum Staff per Shift**:
  - Morning (08:00-14:00): 8 staff
  - Afternoon (14:00-20:00): 12 staff
  - Evening (20:00-close): 10 staff
- **Weekend Multiplier**: 1.5x base staffing
- **Manager on Duty**: Required at all times
- **Maximum Consecutive Hours**: 9 hours per shift with mandatory 1-hour break
- **Staff Training**: Minimum 40 hours initial training + 8 hours quarterly refresher

### 1.3 Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- **Dine-in Service**: Order to table within 12 minutes
- **Drive-thru**: Order to handoff within 5 minutes
- **Takeaway**: Order ready within 10 minutes
- **Delivery**: Order dispatched within 8 minutes, delivered within 35 minutes
- **SLA Breach Threshold**: <90% compliance triggers investigation

---

## 2. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

### 2.1 Stock Level Rules
- **Reorder Point**: 25% of maximum capacity
- **Safety Stock**: 3 days of average consumption
- **Maximum Stock**: No item should exceed 150% of weekly average usage
- **Perishables Shelf Life**:
  - Fresh produce: 48 hours
  - Meat products: 72 hours (refrigerated)
  - Dairy: 5 days
  - Frozen items: 90 days

### 2.2 Ordering Cycles
- **Daily Fresh Produce**: Delivered 06:00-08:00 daily
- **Meat/Proteins**: 3x weekly (Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday)
- **Dry Goods/Packaging**: Weekly (Sunday delivery)
- **Emergency Orders**: Available with 4-hour notice, 15% surcharge

### 2.3 Waste Management
- **Target Waste**: <3% of total inventory value
- **Daily Waste Audit**: Mandatory logging by shift supervisor
- **Disposal Rules**:
  - Food waste: End of shift disposal
  - Expired items: Immediate removal and documentation
  - Damaged packaging: Return to supplier within 24 hours

### 2.4 Temperature Controls
- **Freezer**: -18°C to -22°C (monitored hourly)
- **Refrigerator**: 2°C to 4°C (monitored hourly)
- **Hot Holding**: 60°C minimum (monitored every 2 hours)
- **Temperature Breach**: Immediate escalation and product quarantine

## 3. FINANCIAL RULES

### 3.1 Pricing Strategy
- **Price Change Frequency**: Maximum quarterly
- **Competitor Price Monitoring**: Weekly review mandatory
- **Price Variance Tolerance**: ±5% vs nearest competitor for comparable items
- **Premium Position**: Maintain 10-15% premium over local chains
- **Value Position**: Match or undercut global chains on value items

### 3.2 Discount Authorization
- **Store Manager**: Up to 10% discount
- **Area Manager**: Up to 25% discount
- **Regional Director**: Above 25% discount
- **Complimentary Items**: Manager approval required, logged in system
- **Loyalty Program**: Automatic 5% for registered customers

### 3.3 Payment Processing
- **Cash Handling Limit**: 10,000 SAR per register
- **Daily Deposit**: Mandatory for amounts >15,000 SAR
- **Card Processing Fee**: Absorbed by company
- **Digital Wallet**: Preferred (3% cost saving vs cards)
- **Split Payments**: Allowed up to 2 payment methods

### 3.4 Revenue Targets
- **Daily Target per Store**:
  - S001 (Olaya): 12,500 SAR
  - S002 (Al Malqa): 14,000 SAR
  - S003 (King Fahd): 10,500 SAR
  - S004 (Granada): 13,000 SAR
  - S005 (Riyadh Park): 15,500 SAR
- **Weekend Uplift Target**: +40% vs weekday average
- **Month-end Target**: 95% of monthly budget minimum

## 4. PROMOTIONAL RULES

### 4.1 Promotion Approval
- **Store-level Promotions**: Not allowed without HQ approval
- **Chain-wide Promotions**: Minimum 2-week advance notice
- **Seasonal Promotions**: Planned quarterly, approved 6 weeks in advance
- **Emergency Promotions**: Area Manager approval, 24-hour implementation

### 4.2 Promotion Constraints
- **Maximum Concurrent Promotions**: 3 active promotions per store
- **Minimum Promotion Duration**: 7 days
- **Maximum Discount**: 35% off regular price
- **Blackout Dates**: No promotions during Ramadan first 10 days
- **Stacking**: Customer can use max 1 promotion + loyalty discount

### 4.3 Promotion Performance Tracking
- **Daily Sales Lift**: Tracked against baseline (previous 4 weeks)
- **Target Lift**: 20% minimum sales increase
- **Break-even Margin**: Must maintain 18% gross margin minimum
- **Underperforming Promotion**: Cancelled if <10% lift after 3 days

## 5. FOOD SAFETY & QUALITY

### 5.1 Quality Standards
- **Cooking Temperature**: 
  - Beef patties: 72°C internal temperature
  - Chicken: 75°C internal temperature
  - Oil: 175°C for frying
- **Holding Time**: 
  - Cooked burgers: Maximum 15 minutes
  - Fries: Maximum 7 minutes
  - Assembled burgers: Serve immediately
- **Product Testing**: Random quality checks every 2 hours

### 5.2 Hygiene Requirements
- **Hand Washing**: Every 30 minutes and after each break
- **Glove Change**: Every 1 hour or when contaminated
- **Sanitization**: All surfaces every 2 hours
- **Deep Clean**: Full kitchen daily after close
- **Health Inspections**: Municipal quarterly + internal monthly

### 5.3 Supplier Requirements
- **Approved Suppliers Only**: Pre-vetted and contracted
- **SFDA Certification**: Mandatory for all food suppliers
- **Halal Certification**: 100% of meat/poultry suppliers
- **Quality Audit**: Quarterly supplier facility visits
- **Supplier Performance**: 98% on-time delivery, 99.5% quality acceptance

## 6. CUSTOMER SERVICE

### 6.1 Service Standards
- **Greeting**: Within 30 seconds of customer arrival
- **Order Taking**: Maximum 2 minutes per customer
- **Complaint Response**: Manager intervention within 5 minutes
- **Refund Authorization**: Store manager for amounts up to 150 SAR
- **Customer Satisfaction Target**: 4.2/5.0 minimum average

### 6.2 Queue Management
- **Maximum Wait Time**: 
  - Counter: 5 minutes
  - Drive-thru: 7 minutes (from order to pickup)
- **Queue Length Threshold**: Open additional register if >6 customers waiting
- **Peak Hour Staffing**: All registers operational

### 6.3 Complaint Handling
- **Immediate Resolution**: Empower staff to resolve on-spot (remake/refund)
- **Escalation Matrix**: 
  - Level 1: Crew member (minor issues)
  - Level 2: Shift supervisor (remakes, small refunds)
  - Level 3: Store manager (significant refunds, serious complaints)
- **Follow-up**: Manager callback within 24 hours for serious complaints
- **Compensation Limits**: 
  - Meal remake: Unlimited
  - Refund: Up to order value
  - Goodwill voucher: 50 SAR maximum

## 7. DELIVERY OPERATIONS

### 7.1 Delivery Constraints
- **Delivery Radius**: 5 km from store
- **Minimum Order**: 25 SAR (waived during promotions)
- **Delivery Fee**: 10 SAR (free for orders >50 SAR)
- **Average Delivery Time**: 25-30 minutes
- **Maximum Simultaneous Orders**: 15 per store

### 7.2 Delivery Partner SLAs
- **On-time Delivery**: 90% of orders within promised time
- **Order Accuracy**: 98% correct orders
- **Temperature Maintenance**: Hot food >60°C, cold items <8°C
- **Packaging Integrity**: 99.5% undamaged deliveries
- **Driver Rating**: 4.5/5.0 minimum average

## 10. RISK MANAGEMENT

### 10.1 Food Safety Incidents
- **Immediate Actions**:
  - Stop serving affected product
  - Quarantine all related inventory
  - Notify Area Manager within 15 minutes
  - Document incident completely
- **Investigation**: Complete within 24 hours
- **Corrective Actions**: Implement within 48 hours
- **Customer Communication**: Proactive if public health risk

### 10.2 Equipment Failure
- **Critical Equipment**: Grill, fryer, freezer, POS system
- **Backup Requirements**: 
  - Generator for power outages
  - Manual order system for POS failure
  - Emergency supplier contact for critical equipment
- **Repair SLA**: 4-hour response time for critical equipment
- **Preventive Maintenance**: Monthly inspection schedule

### 10.3 Security Protocols
- **Cash Handling**: Dual custody for amounts >5,000 SAR
- **Safe Limits**: Maximum 30,000 SAR overnight
- **Closing Procedures**: Two-person closing team mandatory
- **Video Surveillance**: 24/7 recording, 90-day retention
- **Incident Reporting**: Immediate for theft, injury, or significant damage

## 12. BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE RULES

### 12.1 Demand Forecasting
- **Forecast Horizon**: 7-day rolling forecast
- **Update Frequency**: Daily based on latest data
- **Factors Considered**: 
  - Historical sales trends
  - Day of week patterns
  - Weather forecasts
  - Promotional calendar
  - Events/holidays
- **Accuracy Target**: ±10% of actual sales

### 12.2 Inventory Optimization
- **Reorder Logic**: Dynamic based on consumption rate + lead time + safety stock
- **Par Levels**: Reviewed monthly, adjusted seasonally
- **Dead Stock**: Items with <5 units sold in 30 days flagged for review
- **Overstock Alert**: Items exceeding 120% of par level

### 12.3 Price Elasticity
- **Price Testing**: Controlled tests in 1-2 stores before rollout
- **Minimum Test Duration**: 14 days
- **Success Criteria**: Revenue increase OR volume increase with maintained margin
- **Rollback Trigger**: >10% volume decrease without revenue compensation
